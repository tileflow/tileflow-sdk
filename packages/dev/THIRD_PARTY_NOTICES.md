## Google Places icons

The glyph artwork in `assets/streets-poi` is adapted from Google Places icons published in the
[Google Places API documentation](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/icons).
Copyright Google LLC. The documentation content is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Tileflow modifies the source artwork by resizing and recoloring the glyphs, then composing them
inside circular category markers with a white rim and shadow.
